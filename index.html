    Why this checklist matters

    Most fall-related injuries happen at home — and most are preventable with the right modifications. A quick assessment is the first step.

    Falls are the #1 cause of injury in older adults

    The CDC estimates 3 million older adults are treated in emergency rooms for falls each year. Most involve common home hazards.

    Small changes have big impact

    Grab bars, better lighting, and removing loose rugs are low-cost fixes that dramatically reduce fall risk.

    Know before you call a contractor

    Understanding your home's risk profile helps you have a more informed conversation with any aging-in-place professional.

    What the credentials mean

    NAHB certifications are earned through coursework, field experience, and continuing education.

    CAPS

    Certified Aging-in-Place Specialist. Trained in home modifications that let people live safely and independently as they age — wider doorways, zero-step entries, grab bars, walk-in showers.

    CGR

    Certified Graduate Remodeler. Demonstrates experience and business skill specific to remodeling projects, from planning through completion.

    CGB

    Certified Graduate Builder. Focused on new-construction expertise, project management, and building-science fundamentals.

    CGP

    Certified Green Professional. Covers energy-efficient, sustainable building and remodeling practices.
